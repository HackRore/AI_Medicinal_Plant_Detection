BOTANICAL NAME: Asparagus racemosus
AYURVEDIC NAME: Shatavari
MEDICINAL PROPERTIES: Galactagogue, Nutritive tonic, Cooling
CLASSICAL USES: Female reproductive health, Gastritis, Immunity, Lactation support
CONTRAINDICATIONS: Avoid in cases of pulmonary congestion.
DOSAGE: 3-6g of root powder with warm milk.
