BOTANICAL NAME: Andrographis paniculata
AYURVEDIC NAME: Kalmegha
MEDICINAL PROPERTIES: Hepatoprotective, Bitter tonic, Antipyretic
CLASSICAL USES: Liver disorders, Fever, Indigestion, Viral infections
CONTRAINDICATIONS: Avoid during pregnancy or if trying to conceive.
DOSAGE: 1-3g of powder or 5-10ml of decoction.
