BOTANICAL NAME: Aloe barbadensis
AYURVEDIC NAME: Kumari
MEDICINAL PROPERTIES: Laxative, Wound healing, Liver tonic
CLASSICAL USES: Skin burns, Constipation, Menstrual irregularities, Liver enlargement
CONTRAINDICATIONS: Not for internal use during pregnancy or breastfeeding.
DOSAGE: 10-20ml of fresh pulp/juice.
