BOTANICAL NAME: Withania somnifera
AYURVEDIC NAME: Ashwagandha
MEDICINAL PROPERTIES: Nervine tonic, Aphrodisiac, Anti-stress, Revitalizer
CLASSICAL USES: Stress, Anxiety, Insomnia, General weakness, Joint pain
CONTRAINDICATIONS: Avoid in cases of severe hyperthyroidism or congestion.
DOSAGE: 3-6g of root powder mixed with warm milk or water.
