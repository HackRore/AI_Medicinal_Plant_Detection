BOTANICAL NAME: Combination (Amla, Bahera, Haritaki)
AYURVEDIC NAME: Triphala
MEDICINAL PROPERTIES: Laxative, Detoxifier, Rejuvenative
CLASSICAL USES: Constipation, Weight loss, Digestion, Eye health
CONTRAINDICATIONS: Avoid during pregnancy or acute diarrhea.
DOSAGE: 3-6g with warm water at bedtime.
