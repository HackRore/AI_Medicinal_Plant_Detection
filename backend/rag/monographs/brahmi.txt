BOTANICAL NAME: Bacopa monnieri
AYURVEDIC NAME: Brahmi
MEDICINAL PROPERTIES: Memory enhancer, Brain tonic, Sedative
CLASSICAL USES: Memory loss, Anxiety, Concentration, Epilepsy, Mental fatigue
CONTRAINDICATIONS: May cause stomach upset if taken on an empty stomach.
DOSAGE: 5-10ml of fresh juice or 1-2g of dried powder.
