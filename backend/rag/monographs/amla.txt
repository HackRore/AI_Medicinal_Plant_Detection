BOTANICAL NAME: Emblica officinalis
AYURVEDIC NAME: Amalaki
MEDICINAL PROPERTIES: Rich in Vitamin C, Antioxidant, Rejuvenative
CLASSICAL USES: Immunity, Hair health, Digestion, Eye health, Premature aging
CONTRAINDICATIONS: Generally very safe. May lower blood sugar.
DOSAGE: 3-6g of powder or 10-20ml of fresh juice.
