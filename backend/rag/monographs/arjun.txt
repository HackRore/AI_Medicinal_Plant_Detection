BOTANICAL NAME: Terminalia arjuna
AYURVEDIC NAME: Arjuna
MEDICINAL PROPERTIES: Cardioprotective, Hypolipidemic, Astringent
CLASSICAL USES: Heart disease, Hypertension, High cholesterol, Fractures
CONTRAINDICATIONS: Generally safe, but consult physician if taking blood thinners.
DOSAGE: 3-6g of bark powder or 10-20ml of decoction.
